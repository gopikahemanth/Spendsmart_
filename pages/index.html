About SpendSmart

SpendSmart is a privacy-focused expense tracking application designed for simplicity and clarity. We believe managing money should be easy, distraction-free, and secure.

No emails. No phone numbers. No unnecessary personal data. Your account is protected with a username and password, and your data stays safe.

Built with clean design principles and modern web technologies, SpendSmart helps you develop better financial habits with confidence.
